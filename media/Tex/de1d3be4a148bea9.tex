\begin{center}
A distribution of opinions is denoted by a pair of $m$-dimensional vectors\\ 
                   $(\vec{v},\vec{p}) = (\langle v_1,\ldots,v_m \rangle,\langle p_1,\ldots,p_m \rangle)\\ 
                   \in [0,1]^m \times \mathbb{N}^m$, where $v_i$ represents the value of opinion $i$,\\
                   and $p_i$ represents the number of individuals holding opinion $i$.
\end{center}
